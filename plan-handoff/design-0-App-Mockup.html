The self-maintaining loop

Six steps, one of which is a human — deliberately. Verification is the product, so approval never gets automated away.

1
Ingest

Scoped connectors pull recent activity from Slack, Drive, GitHub, Jira and Linear on a per-page cadence.

2
Detect drift

Cheap bulk scanners compare each factual claim in a section against decision-bearing events, section by section.

3
Propose

A drafter writes the minimal redline, a one-line rationale and the exact source quote that justifies it.

4
Approve

The page owner sees the diff side by side with its evidence and approves, edits or rejects. Nothing applies itself.

5
Ship & re-verify

The page updates, a version and audit row are written, and the cadence timer resets for the next sweep.

6
Serve

Verified answers only — every response cites its page, section and last-verified date.

Truewiki — always-fresh knowledge for teams that ship. Audit trail · Role-based access · Human in the loop
